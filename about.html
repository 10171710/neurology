Compassionate Neuroscience

Redefining Chronic Cephalic Care Through Accurate Diagnostics

Headache medicine has historically been plagued by rushed 10-minute office visits and generic prescriptions that fail to address root neurochemical pathways. NeuroCare was established with a singular objective: build the Northwest's foremost multidisciplinary center devoted entirely to headache disorders, traumatic brain recovery, and neurophysiology.

Every patient is assigned an integrated clinical team composed of a fellowship neurologist, an interventional pain nurse, and a headache diary navigator to ensure continuous therapeutic alignment.

Our Mission

To eradicate disabling headache episodes through research-backed CGRP, neuromodulation, and personalized neuro-rehabilitation.

Our Vision

A future where neurological care is preventive, immediately accessible via digital telemetry, and zero patients suffer in silence.

Clinical Evolution

Our History of Neurological Milestones

Continuous investments in clinical research and patient-centric healthcare technologies.

2008

Clinic Foundation in Seattle

Established by Dr. Marcus Vance with 3 specialized clinical suites focused on refractive migraine cases and ambulatory EEG diagnostic monitoring.

2014

Multidisciplinary Concussion Lab Launch

Recruited Dr. Alexander Rostov to lead the regional TBI recovery division, installing computerized vestibular platforms and infrared oculomotor tracking suites.

2018

Dedicated Acute Infusion Suite Opening

Constructed private quiet infusion rooms offering same-day CGRP monoclonal antibody infusions and IV rescue protocols as a compassionate hospital ER alternative.

2024 - Present

Tele-Neurology & Continuous Digital Diary Integration

Launched our HIPAA-secure patient portal enabling real-time headache logging, instant neurologist biometric reviews, and nationwide virtual care.

Clinical Infrastructure

Dedicated Neuro-Imaging Labs & Care Teams

Equipped with cutting-edge 3T MRI, electroencephalography suites, and collaborative physician case conference rooms.

High-Field Diagnostic Suite

3T Brain MRI & Advanced Cranial Neuro-Imaging

Equipped with silent scan sequences and ultra-high resolution gradient coils to identify subtle vascular abnormalities, micro-vascular ischemic changes, and intracranial hypertension.

Collaborative Board Reviews

Weekly Complex Migraine & TBI Case Conferences

Fellowship-trained neurologists, physical therapists, and pharmacological specialists meet to review refractory patient profiles and construct unified recovery trajectories.
