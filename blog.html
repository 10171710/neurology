FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How our articles are written, reviewed, and how you can use them. Every piece is rooted in current clinical guidance from our headache and neurology team.

Still have questions? Contact our team
Who writes and reviews the NeuroCare blog articles?
Every article is written or reviewed by our board-certified neurologists and fellowship-trained specialists. Each piece is fact-checked against current clinical guidelines before it is published.
Is this content a substitute for medical advice?
No. Our articles are for education only and cannot replace a personalised evaluation. If you are experiencing new, severe, or sudden headache symptoms, please seek urgent care or book a consultation.
How often do you publish new articles?
New evidence summaries, patient guides, and specialist columns are published every month, with urgent guideline updates posted as soon as they are released.
Can I share or republish an article?
You may share links freely for personal or educational use. For commercial republication or translation, please request written permission through our contact page.
Do you cover both acute and preventive treatments?
Yes. Our library spans acute rescue therapies, preventive medications, CGRP monoclonal antibodies, Botox protocols, neuromodulation, and lifestyle-based approaches to headache care.
How can I suggest a topic or request a correction?
We welcome reader feedback. Send topic suggestions or corrections through our contact form and the editorial board will review them for upcoming articles.
