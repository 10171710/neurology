Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Neurology Visit Questions

What should I bring to my first neurology appointment?
Please bring a photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications, and any prior imaging (MRI or CT discs) and test reports. Completing the online intake form before your visit helps us streamline your consultation.
Do you accept my insurance and how does pre-authorization work?
We are in-network with most major commercial plans, Medicare, and regional carriers. Our billing team verifies benefits and handles pre-authorization for infusions and advanced imaging, and we confirm coverage details before your visit.
How quickly can I get a same-day migraine or infusion appointment?
Acute headache and infusion slots are held daily. Call our urgent line or use the online request form before 2:00 PM and we will typically accommodate a same-day evaluation, with most patients seen within 15 minutes of arrival.
What is CGRP infusion therapy and am I a candidate?
CGRP monoclonal antibodies are preventive treatments for chronic migraine. After a diagnostic intake and headache history review, our specialists determine eligibility and coordinate infusion scheduling in our private treatment suites.
Do you offer tele-neurology video visits?
Yes. Follow-up consultations, medication management, and many second opinions can be completed through our HIPAA-secure video platform. Some conditions still require an in-person exam or diagnostic testing.
How do I access my patient portal and test results?
After your visit you receive a secure invite to the NeuroCare portal, where MRI, EEG, and lab results are released alongside your headache diary and appointment history. Contact portal support if you need access re-enabled.
