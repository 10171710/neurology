Patient Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions About Seeing Our Specialists

Everything you need to know about booking, medical referrals, record transfers, and telehealth appointments.

Do I need a primary care physician referral to see a neurologist?

Most commercial PPO plans and self-pay patients do not require a formal physician referral to book directly with our neurologists. If you have an HMO plan, our clinical intake coordination desk can assist your primary care office in submitting a rapid referral and prior authorization.

What records or imaging scans should I bring to my first appointment?

Please bring any prior brain or cervical MRI/CT radiology reports (and DICOM discs if available), a list of past preventative and acute medications tried, recent routine bloodwork, and your headache log or diary entries. You can also upload these securely via our digital patient portal prior to arrival.

Can I consult with a specialist via Tele-Neurology before traveling?

Yes! We offer HIPAA-compliant video tele-neurology consultations for patients across Washington and neighboring states. Our neurologists can conduct comprehensive clinical history intakes, review imaging remotely, prescribe preventative therapies, and schedule in-person infusion suites or nerve blocks as needed.

How fast can I be seen if I am experiencing an acute intractable flare?

We reserve same-day and 24-hour priority acute slots specifically for status migrainosus, severe cluster headache bursts, and acute concussive episodes to help patients avoid crowded emergency departments. Call our urgent triage desk directly at +1 (800) 555-NEURO.
