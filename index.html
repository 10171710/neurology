PREEMPT Protocol Botox for Migraine

Precision 31-site onabotulinumtoxinA injections across head and neck pain corridors. Paralyzes peripheral pain fibers and prevents sensitization of central brain pain centers.

  • Administered every 12 weeks
  • Over 2.5 million treatments worldwide
  • Approved by Medicare & commercial insurance

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common inquiries regarding consultations, treatments, and insurance.

In most cases, direct self-referrals are welcomed for both in-person visits and tele-neurology consultations. However, certain HMO insurance networks may require a referral from your primary care provider. Our intake coordinators will gladly verify your insurance plan prior to your visit.
Your fellowship-trained neurologist conducts an in-depth cranial nerve examination, motor/sensory testing, review of past MRI/CT scans, trigger mapping, and develops a customized multi-phase treatment protocol that balances acute attack relief with preventive medications.
Yes. Both FDA-approved CGRP monoclonal antibodies and Botox for chronic migraine (defined as 15+ headache days per month) are widely covered. Our dedicated prior-authorization desk handles all paperwork and appeals directly with your insurance provider.
