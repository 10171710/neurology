Billing Questions

Pricing & Coverage FAQ

Straight answers about costs, insurance, and payment options before you commit.

Do the listed plan prices include insurance coverage?
The prices shown are self-pay or concierge rates. If you are in-network, your actual cost is usually just your plan's copay or coinsurance, which is typically far lower. We verify your specific benefits before your first visit.
What happens if my insurance is out of network?
We still see you. Our billing team provides a transparent self-pay estimate up front and can submit out-of-network claims on your behalf for possible reimbursement.
Do you offer payment plans or financial assistance?
Yes. Interest-free installment plans are available on request, and our financial assistance program offers reduced rates for qualifying patients. Ask our billing desk for details.
Are there any hidden fees or surprise charges?
No. You receive an itemized written estimate before treatment begins, and we notify you in advance of any change so there are never surprise invoices.
Is a deposit required to book an appointment?
A refundable deposit is only required for certain procedures and advanced imaging. Routine consultations and follow-up visits do not require a deposit.
Can I switch or cancel my care plan?
Yes. Subscriptions are month-to-month and can be paused or cancelled at any time. You keep full access through the end of your paid period with no exit fees.
