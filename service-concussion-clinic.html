Active Neurological Rehabilitation for Post-Concussion Syndrome

Prolonged dark-room rest after mild traumatic brain injury is outdated and often worsens symptoms. Our concussion protocol applies modern neuro-rehabilitation science: customized sub-threshold aerobic conditioning, computerized convergence and saccadic eye tracking exercises, and specialized vestibular physical therapy. We safely restore autonomic balance and resolve post-traumatic headache and brain fog.

Clinical Indications & Eligibility

Our multidisciplinary concussion recovery clinic treats patients suffering from:

Persistent Post-Concussive Symptoms: Headaches, brain fog, and dizziness lasting more than 14 days following head trauma.
Vestibular-Ocular Dysfunction: Blurred vision when reading, nausea in busy grocery aisles, or motion sensitivity in cars.
Athletic Return-to-Play: Structured step-by-step physical exertion clearance for high school, collegiate, and professional athletes.
Academic & Work Accommodations: Formal neurological documentation for temporary 504 plans and screen-time adjustments.

The Rehabilitation Journey

1

Buffalo Concussion Treadmill Test & VOMS

We determine your exact heart-rate symptom threshold and measure eye-tracking convergence distance.

2

Sub-Symptom Aerobic & Oculomotor Conditioning

Supervised weekly sessions to re-train the brain's autonomic blood-flow autoregulation and vestibular balance systems.

3

Objective Clearance & Return-to-Activity

Repeated neuro-cognitive testing confirms normal brain processing speed and balance before clearance is granted.
