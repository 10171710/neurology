Calcitonin Gene-Related Peptide (CGRP) Targeted Monoclonal Antibody Therapy

Calcitonin Gene-Related Peptide (CGRP) is a potent neuropeptide released during acute migraine attacks that causes painful intracranial vasodilation and mast cell neuro-inflammation. By administering targeted monoclonal antibodies via intravenous infusion, CGRP receptors are directly blocked, preventing migraine cascade initiation before head pain manifests.

Clinical Indications & Eligibility

Our clinical protocol is formulated for adult patients meeting international criteria for refractory migraine:

Chronic Migraine Diagnosis: 15 or more headache days per month for at least 3 consecutive months.
Prior Medication Failures: Inadequate response or intolerable side effects from oral triptans, topiramate, or beta-blockers.
Cluster Headache Cycles: Episodic or chronic trigeminal autonomic cephalalgia unresponsive to high-flow oxygen.
Medication Overuse Risk: Seeking a preventive non-sedating therapy to safely discontinue daily NSAIDs or opioids.

The Infusion Suite Experience

Designed from the ground up for patients experiencing photophobia and phonophobia (extreme light and sound sensitivity):

One-on-one infusion monitoring by certified neuro-registered nurses Continuous Vital Checks
1

Arrival & Private Darkened Suite Intake

Our nurses greet you in a softly illuminated environment with white-noise dampening. Pre-infusion blood pressure, temperature, and headache severity scores are logged.

2

Intravenous Administration (30 Minutes)

100mg or 300mg of eptinezumab-jjmr (Vyepti) is infused through a fine gauge catheter with warm saline. Heated blankets and ergonomic orthopedic recliners ensure absolute comfort.

3

15-Minute Observation & Diary Sync

Following completion, post-infusion vitals are checked. Your smartphone patient diary is synchronized to automatically monitor attack frequencies over the next 90 days.
