PREEMPT Protocol OnabotulinumtoxinA 31-Point Neuromuscular Injections

The PREEMPT (Phase 3 REsearch Evaluating Migraine Prophylaxis Therapy) clinical protocol delivers 155 units of onabotulinumtoxinA across 31 precise anatomical sites across 7 specific muscle zones (corrugator, procerus, frontalis, temporalis, occipitalis, cervical paraspinals, and upper trapezius). Rather than relaxing facial muscles for cosmetic purposes, it inhibits peripheral sensory pain fibers from transmitting nociceptive signals back to the central trigeminal brainstem nucleus.

Clinical Indications & Eligibility

Botox is clinically approved and covered by insurance for patients meeting the following neurological criteria:

Chronic Migraine Pattern: 15 or more headache days per month for ≥3 months, with at least 8 fulfilling migraine criteria.
Medication Intolerance: Lack of adequate prophylactic relief from daily preventive pills (topiramate, beta blockers, tricyclics).
Cranio-Cervical Myofascial Spasms: Persistent muscle contraction in neck, trapezius, and occipital regions exacerbating migraines.
Insurance Pre-Authorization: Full clinical documentation provided for commercial plans and Medicare.

The Treatment Experience

Fast, gentle, and performed exclusively by board-certified headache specialists in comfortable, quiet rooms:

1

Anatomical Mapping & Cold Compresses

Our neurologist identifies your specific pain corridors and applies gentle cooling to minimize pinch sensations.

2

31-Point Micro-Injections (15 Minutes)

Using microscopic 30-gauge needles, 5 units are placed at each standardized PREEMPT point across forehead, temples, occiput, and upper shoulders.

3

Immediate Return to Normal Routine

There is no downtime. Relief begins within 10 to 14 days and builds progressively over subsequent 12-week injection cycles.

Frequently Asked Questions: Botox Protocol

Most patients report only mild, brief pinprick sensations lasting 2–3 seconds per site. We use the finest gauge needles available and offer topical numbing or cold packs upon request.
Clinical guidelines recommend completing 2 cycles (24 weeks) because Botox exhibits cumulative therapeutic benefits. Many patients experience substantial reductions in severity by Cycle 1.
Most commercial insurance plans and Medicare cover PREEMPT Botox for chronic migraine patients. Combined with manufacturer savings programs, patient out-of-pocket costs are often as low as $0 to $50 per session.
