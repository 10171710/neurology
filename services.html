Coverage & Access

Insurance Coverage & Prior-Authorization Support

Navigating insurance approvals for advanced neurological biologics and diagnostics shouldn't be stressful. Our dedicated in-house coordinators manage all paperwork from initial filing to approval.

Step One

Medical Necessity Documentation

Our clinical team gathers and formats your complete treatment history, monthly attack counts, and prior oral medication trials to satisfy strict insurance criteria for specialty approvals and appeals.

Required Criteria: Diagnosis • 90-day diary • Oral trial documentation
Step Two

Expedited Prior Authorization Support

Our full-time insurance liaisons submit and monitor claims directly with Blue Cross Blue Shield, Aetna, UnitedHealthcare, Cigna, and Medicare. Peer-to-peer reviews are handled personally by our neurologists.

Average Processing Time: 48 to 72 business hours for primary decisions
Step Three

Co-Pay & Foundation Assistance

For FDA-approved therapies such as Botox and CGRP infusions, we connect insured patients with manufacturer co-pay programs that lower patient out-of-pocket costs to $0–$50 per treatment.

Patient Savings: Up to $4,000+ per year in co-pay support
